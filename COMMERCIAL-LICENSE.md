## When the AGPL is enough

Anyone can use, study, modify and redistribute GreenLens under the AGPL at no
cost, inside a company as well, as long as its terms are met. In short, and
without replacing the license text:

- Distributing GreenLens, modified or not, requires providing its source code
  under the AGPL.
- Modifying GreenLens and letting users interact with it over a network
  requires offering those users the source code of the modified version
  (section 13 of the AGPL).

Running an unmodified GreenLens on your own machines to measure your own
workloads does not by itself require publishing anything.

## When a commercial license is needed

A commercial license replaces the AGPL obligations with contractual terms. It
fits cases such as:

- offering a modified GreenLens as a network service without publishing the
  modifications;
- shipping GreenLens, or parts of it, inside proprietary software or
  appliances;
- internal policies that do not allow software under the AGPL.

describe the organization, the intended use and the number of nodes. Terms are
