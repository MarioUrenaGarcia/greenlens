GreenLens is offered under two licenses.

The **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 only** covers every feature.
There is no paid edition with extra features. Under the AGPL you can use,
study, modify and redistribute GreenLens at no cost, inside a company as well.
If you distribute it, or let users interact with a modified version over a
network, you must provide the corresponding source code.

A **commercial license** replaces those obligations with contractual terms,
for organizations that ship GreenLens inside proprietary products or offer a
modified version as a service without publishing the changes.

Offering both licenses requires that the copyright holder can relicense every
contribution. For that reason, contributions are accepted under a Contributor
License Agreement, and dependencies are limited to permissive licenses: MIT,
BSD, Apache-2.0 and ISC, plus the SIL Open Font License for fonts.

The full texts are in the repository: `LICENSE`, `COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md` and
`CLA.md`. Third-party components and their licenses are listed in
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
