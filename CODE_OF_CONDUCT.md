# Code of conduct

GreenLens is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels
of experience. Everyone who takes part in the project, in issues, pull
requests, discussions or any other space, is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good intent;
- give technical criticism about the work, never about the person;
- accept that decisions are explained and can be questioned with arguments;
- help newcomers find their way.

The following is not accepted: harassment, insults, discrimination of any
kind, sexual language or imagery, threats, and publishing private information
of others without their consent.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer through
https://github.com/MarioUrenaGarcia. Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and may temporarily or
permanently ban anyone whose behavior breaks this code.
